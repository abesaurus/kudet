Why Hybrid Cash

Your money never sleeps

Most cards let you spend. Hybrid Cash lets you earn while you wait — then spend whenever you're ready. The "hybrid" is crypto and cash working as one.

01

Earn on every dollar

Your loaded balance is deployed into lending protocols automatically and compounds in the background. Idle money stops being idle — until you swipe, it's earning.

02

One balance, any asset

ETH, USDG, or tokenized stocks and ETFs on Robinhood Chain all feed a single card balance. No conversion juggling, no off-ramp, no separate accounts.

03

Yield pauses when you pay

Spending is exact to the cent. The moment a transaction clears, the yield on that amount settles and the rest keeps earning — no rounding, no surprise fees.

04

Programmable limits

Set per-transaction and monthly caps, freeze and unfreeze instantly, and get a notification on every single purchase — all onchain, all verifiable.

05

Spend-first, never in debt

You can only spend what you've loaded plus what your balance has earned. No overdrafts, no credit checks, no surprises at the till.

06

Settled on Robinhood Chain

Every load, every yield payment, every top-up is an onchain transaction you can verify yourself. Low fees, fast finality, full transparency.
