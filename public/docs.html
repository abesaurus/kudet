Everything you need to know about the card that earns while you spend.

01 What is Hybrid Cash?

Hybrid Cash is a self-custodial virtual card built on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Connect your wallet, load crypto, and get a virtual card whose balance keeps earning yield in the background until you spend it.

02 Getting a card

  1. Connect wallet — click GET CARD and connect any EVM wallet (e.g. Robinhood Wallet, MetaMask).
  2. Sign in — a gas-free signature saves your address so your card persists across visits.
  3. Deposit — load USDG, ETH, or tokenized stocks as collateral.
  4. Get your card — your virtual card is generated instantly after confirmation.
Fees
New card: $5 one-time · Top-up: $0 · Gas: $0

03 Supported assets

  • USDG — Global Dollar, stablecoin (6 decimals)
  • ETH — native Ether
  • WETH, tokenized stocks (NVDA, TSLA, AAPL)

04 How yield works

Your loaded balance is deployed into on-chain lending markets automatically, earning variable yield right up to the moment you spend. Yield is not guaranteed and fluctuates with market conditions.

05 Cards & security

Card numbers, CVV, and expiry are deterministically generated from your card ID — never stored server-side. You control the card; we never touch your private keys. Virtual cards are simulated representations until issuance by a licensed partner.
